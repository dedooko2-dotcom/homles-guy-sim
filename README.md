# Homeless Guy Survival Simulator

You are a homeless person on the streets. **Survive is the key!**

## About

A text-based survival simulation game where you play as a homeless person trying to survive on the streets. Make strategic decisions to find food, shelter, and earn money while managing your health, hunger, energy, and warmth.

## How to Play

```bash
python3 game.py
```

## Game Mechanics

### Stats to Manage
- **Health** (0-100): If it drops to 0, game over
- **Hunger** (0-100): Decreases daily, starving damages health
- **Energy** (0-100): Required for most actions
- **Warmth** (0-100): Affected by weather and shelter

### Actions
1. **Beg for money** - Ask passersby for spare change
2. **Search for food** - Look for food in dumpsters and around the city
3. **Find shelter** - Find a safe place to sleep
4. **Rest** - Recover energy (better with shelter)
5. **Do odd jobs** - Work small jobs for better pay
6. **Collect cans/bottles** - Gather recyclables for money
7. **Check status** - See your current stats
8. **End day** - Move to the next day

### Win Condition
Survive 30 days and save $100 to get back on your feet!

### Tips
- Always find shelter before ending the day
- Don't let your energy drop too low
- Balance food searching with earning money
- Random events can help or hurt - be prepared!

## Requirements
- Python 3.6+

## Note
This game is meant to raise awareness about homelessness. If you'd like to help, consider supporting local homeless shelters and food banks in your community.
